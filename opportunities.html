My group is part of the Media and Machines Laboratory, which I share with Tao Ju, Caitlin Kelleher, Dennis Cosgrove and Yasu Furukawa. We are always looking for outstanding students at all levels, including students with backgrounds outside of computer science.

Current undergraduate students looking for research opportunities, or current MS students looking for project or thesis ideas should e-mail or drop by my office to discuss current opportunities. Most likely I will ask you to demo or explain some previous projects that you've done. If you haven't had the chance to work on many projects in the past, click here.

Students seeking admission to the PhD program should apply directly to our graduate program. If you mention my name anywhere in your application, it will be forwarded to me.
